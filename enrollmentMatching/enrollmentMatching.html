UVA Enrollment as a Matching Market

Overview




Course enrollment as an economic game with courses and students as players. We consider Random Serial Dictatorship (RSD) and Deferred Acceptance (DA) as mechanisms to optimize matching stability and efficiency, considering the context of course enrollment with many students to one course. We model student and course preferences from course rating platforms and vectorized preference orderings. We show that DA using our preference ordering outperforms RSD, highlighting an opportunity for the University of Virginia (UVA) to improve satisfaction in course enrollment mechanisms.
